Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Still unsure about something?

support@kayantv.app
Does Kayan TV provide channels or subscriptions?＋

No. Kayan TV is a media player only. It does not provide, sell, host, recommend or supply channels, playlists, movies, series or IPTV subscriptions.

Does Kayan TV include any content?＋

Only a small set of open-source demonstration videos is included so you can test the player. These are technical samples—not channels—and nothing else is supplied.

Which devices support Kayan TV?＋

Kayan TV is being built for iPhone and iPad, Android mobile devices and Android TV. We do not list platforms that have not entered development.

Is Premium a subscription?＋

No. Premium is a $9.99 lifetime purchase per platform app. The iOS and Google platform apps are purchased separately.

Can I restore my purchase?＋

Yes. Restore Premium through the same Apple or Google account used for the original purchase on that platform.

Is Kayan TV legal?＋

Kayan TV is a player. You must use sources and media you are authorised to access and follow the laws and agreements that apply to you.
