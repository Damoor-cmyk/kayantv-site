Frequently asked

Straight answers.

support@kayantv.app
Is Kayan TV an IPTV app?＋

Kayan TV is an IPTV player. It plays compatible sources you provide and are authorised to use. It does not supply channels, playlists, subscriptions, movies or live streams.

Does Kayan TV provide IPTV channels?＋

No. Kayan TV is player software only. No channels or commercial media are included.

Does Kayan TV include any content?＋

Only a few open-source demonstration videos—Apple’s public test streams and Blender open movies—so you can see how the player works. Nothing else.

Does Kayan TV support M3U and Xtream Codes?＋

Yes. Kayan TV supports compatible M3U and Xtream Codes sources that you provide and are authorised to use.

Which devices will support Kayan TV?＋

Kayan TV is designed for iPhone and iPad, Android phones and tablets, Android TV and Windows PCs.

Is Kayan TV free?＋

Kayan TV includes free player features. Premium unlocks the complete player for a one-time $9.99 purchase per platform app. No channels or subscriptions are included.
