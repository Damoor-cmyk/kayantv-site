Questions, answered

The important details, upfront.

Does Kayan TV provide channels?

No. Kayan TV is a media player only. It does not provide, sell, host, recommend, or supply channels, playlists, movies, series, or IPTV subscriptions.

Does Kayan TV include any content?

Only a small group of open-source demonstration videos, including public HLS test streams and Blender Foundation open movies, so you can see how the player works. Nothing else is included.

Is it legal?

Kayan TV is a player. You must use sources that you are authorised to access and comply with the rules that apply to you.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. Restore it through the same Apple or Google account used to purchase the app for that platform.
